Affiliates & creators

How BOKKO sources affiliates and creators

Sourcing means finding the affiliates and creators who will sell a product well, then matching each one to the right product and the right story. BOKKO does this for marketplace stores and for new product launches.

Brand suitability

Every affiliate and creator is reviewed against the brand before being approached: the tone and quality of their content, the products they already promote and how credible they are in the category. A creator who does not suit the brand is left out, whatever the size of their following.

Matching to product and buyer

Creators are matched to the product and to the people who buy it: where their audience is, its age and interests, the platform they sell on and the price range their followers respond to. Each product is paired with the creators most likely to sell it.

Storytelling

Each creator is briefed on the story of the product: what it is, who it is for and why it is worth buying. They tell that story in their own voice, so the content feels genuine and still says what the brand needs it to say.

Negotiation and briefing

BOKKO handles the negotiation, the brief, the deliverables, posting dates and usage rights, so the brand works with one team and not with each creator individually.

Affiliates for campaigns and launches

Affiliates are recruited, briefed and kept active around marketplace campaigns and product launches, so more of the right people are selling the product at the moments that matter.

Advertising and reporting

Creator content that performs is supported with advertising where usage rights have been agreed, and affiliates and creators are reported on the sales and reach they bring.
