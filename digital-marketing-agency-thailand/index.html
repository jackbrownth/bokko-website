Process

How we work with a brand

1. Review

We look at the business, the figures you can share and your current channels and ad accounts, and agree what the marketing has to achieve.

2. Plan

Each month we prepare a content calendar and campaign plan built around that objective.

3. Produce and run

Our team shoots the content, publishes it, coordinates creators and manages the campaigns.

4. Report

A monthly report shows what was published, what was spent and what came back.

FAQ

Digital marketing questions

Does BOKKO only work with hospitality brands?

No. Hospitality is the core of BOKKO’s work, and it also works with brands in fashion, beauty, food, real estate, electronics, travel and retail. Clients include Thai Airways, FUNDAO, The Residences at Sindhorn Kempinski Hotel Bangkok, BNT, Moonshot Skincare, Bigen, Little Farm, Italasia, Town Hall Sukhumvit 49, Chabatree and Swing Society.

Which industries has BOKKO worked in outside hospitality?

Travel (Thai Airways), fashion (FUNDAO), beauty and personal care (Moonshot Skincare and Bigen), food and drink (Little Farm and Italasia), real estate (The Residences at Sindhorn Kempinski Hotel Bangkok), electronics and lighting (BNT), retail and events (Town Hall Sukhumvit 49), homeware (Chabatree) and golf and leisure (Swing Society).

Does BOKKO work with clients outside Bangkok, or outside Thailand?

Yes. BOKKO works with clients across Thailand, from its office in Nonthaburi, Greater Bangkok. Content management and paid advertising management are also available to clients outside Thailand.

Is the approach different outside hospitality?

The method is the same: agree the commercial objective, produce content for it, advertise to the right audience and measure what comes back. The audiences, platforms and offers change with the industry.

What does a full-service digital marketing agency do?

It plans and runs a brand’s marketing across channels. At BOKKO that means strategy, photography and video, social media management, creator campaigns and Meta, Google and TikTok advertising, with search, website and e-commerce work where needed.

How much does digital marketing cost with BOKKO?

Programmes are quoted to the brand and scope. BOKKO’s main monthly package is ฿85,000 + VAT, based on a six-month partnership, and one-off and shorter projects are also available.

Who manages the advertising?

BOKKO’s founder, Jack Brown, remains personally involved in the strategy, management and optimisation of advertising campaigns, supported by an in-house team.
