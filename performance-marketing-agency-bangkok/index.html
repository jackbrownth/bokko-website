Process

How campaigns are run

1. Objective

We agree the commercial target first: sales, bookings or qualified enquiries, and what each one is worth.

2. Build

Campaign structure, audiences, tracking and creative are prepared together.

3. Test and optimise

Creative and audiences are tested, and the budget moves to what performs, every month.

4. Report

A monthly report shows spend, results and what they mean for the business.

FAQ

Performance marketing questions

What is a performance marketing agency?

An agency whose work is judged by commercial results such as sales, bookings and qualified enquiries, not by reach or followers. BOKKO runs paid campaigns on Meta, Google and TikTok and reports them against those results.

What results has BOKKO achieved with paid advertising?

Published results include a 131x peak ROAS and more than ฿6 million in sales within 72 hours for Mandarin Oriental Bangkok, a 114x peak ROAS and more than ฿50 million in sales within four months for Thai Airways, and more than ฿2 million in sales within 72 hours for Mandarin Oriental Shops.

Does BOKKO charge a percentage of ad spend?

No. In BOKKO’s monthly packages, advertising management is included with no management fee, and the advertising budget is paid directly to the platforms.

Does BOKKO make the ad creative as well?

Yes. Photography, video and campaign assets are produced by BOKKO’s in-house production team, so the creative and the media plan are built together.

Who manages the campaigns?

BOKKO’s founder, Jack Brown, remains personally involved in the strategy, management and optimisation of advertising campaigns, supported by an in-house team.

Which kinds of brand does BOKKO run paid campaigns for?

Hospitality, travel, e-commerce and beauty brands, among others. Clients include Mandarin Oriental Bangkok, Thai Airways and Bigen.
