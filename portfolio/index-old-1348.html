Ways to work together

Production only,
or the whole system.

Some brands only need the film. Others want the media behind it too. Both are normal, and you can start with the first and add the second later.

Option one

Production only

We plan, shoot and deliver. Your own team publishes.

  • A mood and concept board for every property before anything is filmed — palette, tone, framing, subject, pace. It is the step that decides whether the content looks considered or assembled.
  • One visual identity across every branch. Properties under the same brand get a shared direction, so a group reads as one name rather than several hotels each drifting in their own direction.
  • Photography and short-form video shot by our own crew and equipment, graded and cut for feed, reels and paid placements.
  • Delivered as finished assets in the formats your team already works in, ready to schedule.

Suits a property with a capable in-house social team that needs the craft, not the management.

Option two

Full scope

Everything above, and we run it.

  • Content strategy and publishing. Pillars, calendar, copywriting and community management in Thai and English.
  • Paid media across Meta, TikTok, Google and Google Maps, built around bookings, covers and footfall rather than reach.
  • Creators and KOLs from a network of 3,000+, matched and briefed to the property instead of booked on follower count.
  • Monthly reporting against revenue and visits, with the direction adjusted on what the numbers actually show.

The creative and the media budget sit with the same team, so nothing is lost in the handover between them.
