How we plan and run Meta campaigns

Start with the action you want

A restaurant may want booking enquiries. A hotel may want package sales. A lifestyle product may be better judged on tracked purchases. The objective changes the campaign structure, the creative and what should be measured.

We map the full customer path before running ads, including landing-page quality, contact options, follow-up speed and lead qualification.

Creative is a performance variable

Because we produce content in-house, reels, photography, carousels and messages are planned for the audience and placement. Testing different angles teaches more than endlessly adjusting interest targeting around one weak creative idea.

The production plan considers both brand consistency and the need for new assets as the campaign learns.

Account structure, measurement and iteration

A working plan covers geographic targeting, language, budgets, campaign and ad-set structure, conversion events, exclusions, tracking parameters and reporting. The exact set-up depends on the available data and account access.

We monitor cost and quality, and discuss what the figures actually mean. For lead generation, a low cost per lead is only useful if the enquiries convert.

How BOKKO fits into your team

We combine planning, production and management so briefs are not passed between several unrelated vendors. Your team still provides offer approvals, product knowledge and feedback on lead or booking outcomes.

Meta ad management is included in our standard monthly package, with no separate ad management fee. You set the advertising budget; see current pricing.

FAQ

Meta ads questions

Do you run both Facebook and Instagram ads?

Yes. Both run through Meta’s advertising platform and we manage them together.

Can you produce the ad creatives?

Yes. Photography, short-form video and creative planning are done by our own team.

How does the advertising budget work?

You set the budget and it goes directly to the platform. Ad management is included in the monthly fee, with no management fee and no percentage of the budget.

Can you guarantee leads or sales?

No. No responsible agency can guarantee a specific number of results before testing the market, offer, tracking and sales process.
