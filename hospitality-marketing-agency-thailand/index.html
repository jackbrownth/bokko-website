FAQ

Hospitality marketing questions

What does a hospitality marketing agency do?

It plans and runs the marketing for businesses that sell an experience: restaurants, hotels, resorts, bars and venues. At BOKKO that covers strategy, photography and video, social media management, creator campaigns and Meta, Google and TikTok advertising, reported against bookings, enquiries and revenue.

Which hospitality businesses does BOKKO work with in Thailand?

Restaurants and restaurant groups, hotels and resorts, hotel restaurants and bars, beach clubs, rooftops, cafés and travel brands. Published clients include Mandarin Oriental Bangkok, Thai Airways, Opera Italian Restaurant, Saneh Jaan, Pi Samui Beach Club and Renaissance Pattaya Resort & Spa.

Does BOKKO work with hospitality businesses outside Bangkok?

Yes. BOKKO has worked with venues in Khao Yai, Pattaya, Hua Hin, Koh Samui, Krabi and Samut Prakan as well as Bangkok.

How is hospitality marketing measured?

By the actions that lead to revenue: reservations, calls, direction requests, enquiries, leads and, where the business shares it, sales. Reach and follower numbers are reported but are not the target.

How much does hospitality marketing cost with BOKKO?

BOKKO Hospitality Growth is ฿85,000 + VAT per month, based on a six-month partnership. It includes content production, social media management and Meta, Google and TikTok advertising management with no ad management fee.

Who manages the advertising?

BOKKO’s founder, Jack Brown, remains personally involved in the strategy, management and optimisation of advertising campaigns, supported by an in-house team.
