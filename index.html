Questions we are asked most.

Straight answers on price, how we work and who we work with.

What does BOKKO do?

BOKKO is a revenue-focused marketing agency in Bangkok with its own in-house production team. One team handles creative direction, photography and film, social media management, creator and affiliate campaigns, performance advertising on Meta, Google and TikTok, e-commerce management, SEO and AI search optimisation, and website development. BOKKO is best known for hospitality and also works with travel, fashion, beauty, real estate and retail brands.

What makes BOKKO different from other marketing agencies in Bangkok?

Three things. BOKKO has its own production team, so creative direction, photography, film and editing are done in-house by the people who plan the campaign. Its work is measured in revenue, bookings and sales, not reach: results include a 131x peak ROAS for Mandarin Oriental Bangkok and more than ฿50 million in sales within four months for Thai Airways. And creative, social media, performance advertising, e-commerce, SEO and websites are handled by one team, not passed between several suppliers.

How much experience does BOKKO have?

BOKKO draws on 10 years of marketing experience, has worked with more than 80 clients and has managed more than 200 million baht in advertising spend. Its record campaign return is 131x ROAS, from a Mandarin Oriental Bangkok campaign that generated more than ฿6 million in sales within 72 hours.

Who does BOKKO work with?

Restaurants, hotels, bars, beach clubs and cafés are the core of BOKKO’s work, from independent venues to Mandarin Oriental Bangkok. BOKKO also works with brands in other industries, including Thai Airways (travel), FUNDAO (fashion), The Residences at Sindhorn Kempinski Hotel Bangkok (real estate), BNT (electronics and lighting), Moonshot Skincare and Bigen (beauty), and Little Farm and Italasia (food and drink), and offers a dedicated E-Commerce Growth Management package.

Where does BOKKO work?

Across Thailand. BOKKO’s office is in Nonthaburi, Greater Bangkok, and the team works with clients nationwide, including Bangkok, Khao Yai, Pattaya and Koh Samui. Content management and paid advertising management are also available to clients outside Thailand.

How much does BOKKO cost?

BOKKO Hospitality Growth, the main package for restaurants, hotels and venues, is ฿85,000 + VAT per month, based on a six-month partnership. Signature Content Production, a one-off full-day photo and video shoot, is ฿85,000 + VAT. E-Commerce Growth Management starts from ฿120,000 + VAT per month. One-off projects and shorter engagements are also available.

Do we have to commit to six months?

No. The ฿85,000 monthly price is based on a six-month partnership, which gives time for four professional shoots and for campaigns to build: Opera Italian Restaurant reached three times its monthly revenue in the fourth month. BOKKO also takes on one-off productions and shorter projects.

Is there a fee for managing our advertising?

No. Advertising strategy, setup and management on Meta, Google and TikTok are part of the package, with no management fee and no percentage of your budget. If you choose to advertise, the budget is yours to set and goes straight to the platforms.

How quickly do results show?

It depends on the objective and the starting point. In BOKKO’s published case studies, Pi Samui Beach Club tripled monthly revenue within two months, Opera Italian Restaurant within four months, and a Mandarin Oriental Bangkok campaign generated more than ฿6 million in sales in 72 hours.

How does working with BOKKO start?

With a review of the business, the figures you can share and your current channels and ad accounts, to agree what the marketing has to achieve. Each month BOKKO then prepares a content calendar and campaign plan, shoots the photography and video in-house, publishes and runs the campaigns, and sends a report showing what was published, what was spent and what came back.
