About BOKKO · Digital marketing agency, Thailand

Creative-led marketing. Commercial results in focus.

BOKKO is a revenue-focused marketing agency in Bangkok with its own in-house production team, working with hospitality, travel, fashion, beauty, real estate and retail brands.

We bring strategy, professional content production, social media management and performance advertising together under one team. Our approach combines strong creative thinking with data-driven decisions, connecting brand building with measurable business objectives.

Founded by Jack Brown, BOKKO draws on 10 years of marketing experience to help businesses strengthen their market presence, attract the right audiences and turn attention into enquiries, bookings and sales.

Let’s Talk Explore Our Work
Founder & Director

Jack Brown

Founder of BOKKO

Jack Brown founded BOKKO with a clear philosophy: creative excellence and commercial performance should work hand in hand.

With 10 years of experience in Thailand’s hospitality and digital marketing industries, Jack has worked across restaurants, luxury hotels, travel and lifestyle brands, bringing together strategic thinking, creative direction and performance marketing.

His experience spans brand positioning, content strategy, campaign development and paid media, with a particular focus on connecting marketing activity to meaningful business outcomes.

Hands-on Leadership. Performance-Driven Thinking.

Jack remains personally involved in the strategy, management and optimisation of BOKKO’s advertising campaigns across Meta, Google and TikTok.

This direct involvement ensures that advertising decisions are guided by an understanding of each client’s business, audience and commercial objectives—not simply platform metrics.

Effective performance marketing requires more than campaign setup. It involves understanding customer behaviour, identifying opportunities, testing creative approaches and knowing when to adjust strategy or investment.

At BOKKO, this experience shapes how campaigns are planned, managed and evaluated, supported by an in-house team responsible for strategy, creative production and day-to-day execution.

For clients, the result is a more connected approach to marketing, with experienced leadership, clear accountability and a consistent focus on performance.

Our approach

How creative and commercial thinking work together

Every activity starts with a commercial objective

Before content is planned, we agree what the business needs: weekday covers, room nights, private-event enquiries or a successful launch. The content calendar and campaigns are then built to deliver it.

Results are published with the client named

Our case studies give the client, the period and the outcome. Opera Italian Restaurant grew from ฿4m to ฿12m a month in four months, and Pi Samui Beach Club from ฿2m to ฿6m a month in two.

Content and advertising are planned by one team

Photography, video, publishing, creator campaigns and paid media are planned together, so each shoot produces what the campaigns need and each campaign leads somewhere people can enquire or book.

Hospitality is our specialism

Restaurants, hotels, bars, beach clubs, cafés and travel brands make up the core of our work, from independent venues to Mandarin Oriental Bangkok and Thai Airways. For new venues, our hospitality advisory service covers feasibility, pre-opening and operations.

Process

FAQ

Questions about BOKKO

What is BOKKO?

BOKKO, also known as BOKKO Marketing Specialists, is a Bangkok-based digital marketing agency specialising in hospitality, restaurants, lifestyle and growth-focused brands. It brings strategy, professional content production, social media management and performance advertising together under one team. The registered company is BOKKO Digital Co., Ltd. (company registration number 0125566023907).

Who founded BOKKO?

BOKKO was founded by Jack Brown, who has 10 years of experience in Thailand’s hospitality and digital marketing industries, working across restaurants, luxury hotels, travel and lifestyle brands.

Who manages the advertising at BOKKO?

Founder Jack Brown remains personally involved in the strategy, management and optimisation of BOKKO’s advertising campaigns across Meta, Google and TikTok, supported by an in-house team responsible for strategy, creative production and day-to-day execution.

Where is BOKKO based?

BOKKO’s office is at 59/19 T59 Westgate, Rattanathibet Road, Bang Rak Phatthana, Bang Bua Thong, Nonthaburi 11110, Thailand, in Greater Bangkok. The agency works with clients across Thailand, including Bangkok, Khao Yai, Pattaya, Hua Hin and Koh Samui.

What types of business does BOKKO work with?

Mainly restaurants, hotels, bars, beach clubs, cafés, travel and lifestyle brands. Published client work includes Mandarin Oriental Bangkok, Thai Airways, Opera Italian Restaurant, Pi Samui Beach Club and CHON at The Siam. BOKKO also manages e-commerce growth for consumer brands.

How much does BOKKO cost?

BOKKO Hospitality Growth is ฿85,000 + VAT per month, based on a six-month partnership. It includes Meta, Google and TikTok advertising management with no separate ad management fee. One-off and shorter projects are also available. Full details are on the pricing page.

Does BOKKO work in Thai and English?

Yes. BOKKO works with clients, and produces content and campaigns, in both Thai and English.
