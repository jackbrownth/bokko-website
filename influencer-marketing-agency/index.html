How we plan influencer campaigns

Select creators for fit, not follower counts

A creator with a smaller, relevant audience can be more useful than a celebrity whose followers do not match the target guest or customer. We consider audience location, tone, content quality, credibility and the experience the creator can communicate.

For hospitality brands, we also consider whether the creator can explain the venue in a way that gives people a real reason to visit.

Plan the collaboration properly

Creator work needs a clear brief, timing, deliverables, approval process, posting dates and content-rights arrangements. Paid usage rights, exclusivity and boosting are agreed explicitly, never assumed.

A consistent production and campaign calendar helps the collaboration support new menus, openings, offers, events or brand launches.

Connect influencer activity to the rest of the campaign

The best creator post may be only one step in a broader sequence. Relevant brand content, a strong Instagram profile, reservation links and paid media help interested audiences take action.

We set realistic metrics before launch, such as qualified visits, trackable enquiries, coupon use or content reach, and report the limits of attribution.

Quality and transparency

Creator selection and campaign planning follow the relevant advertising disclosure and platform rules. Partners and scope are chosen for the project, with no promise of guaranteed performance.

Creator and KOL campaigns are an optional add-on to our packages, scoped separately for each campaign.

Influencer marketing questions

What is a KOL?

KOL stands for key opinion leader: a person whose expertise or audience influence can shape opinions in a particular market.

Which restaurants in Bangkok has BOKKO run influencer and KOL campaigns for?

Published examples include Jae Bank at Emsphere, Tasca Sabio in Surawong, Saneh Jaan, HONE izakaya on Sukhumvit 24 and บู๊ลิ้มหม้อไฟ เมืองโบราณ, where production, content, creators and multi-channel advertising increased sales by more than 250% within four months. Yes. Creators are chosen for each restaurant by audience location, content quality, credibility and fit with the cuisine and price point, not by follower count alone. No. Creator fees, production requirements, paid amplification and usage rights are itemised in the campaign proposal.

Can influencer content be reused in paid ads?

Often, yes, but paid usage and licensing rights must be agreed explicitly with each creator.
