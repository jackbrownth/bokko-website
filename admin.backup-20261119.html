Enquiries

Every enquiry sent through the website forms, with the ad or page it came from. Open one to update its status, set an appointment, log calls and emails, and keep notes. You still get the usual email for each new one.

For leads that did not come through a website form: a Meta Instant Form, a phone call, LINE or a referral.

Other emails

People who wrote to hello@bokko.co and are not in Enquiries yet. Turn one into a lead, attach it to a lead you already have, or dismiss it. Dismissing only takes it off this list: the email stays in Gmail. Anything left here is cleared after 30 days.

Portfolio access

Email connection

Connect the leads mailbox once. After that, emails to and from each lead appear inside the lead, and you can reply from there. New email is collected every two minutes.

Automatic confirmation
