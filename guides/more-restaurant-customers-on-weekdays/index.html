How to get more customers into a Bangkok restaurant on weekdays

Short answer

Pick one weekday occasion to win, such as office lunch, after-work drinks or early-week dinner. Give people a reason that only exists on those days, put it in front of people who are nearby at the time they decide, and measure weekday covers separately from weekends.

By Jack Brown, Founder of BOKKO · November 2026

1. Choose the occasion

“More weekday customers” is too broad to plan around. A set lunch for nearby offices, a Tuesday tasting menu and a private room for team dinners need different content, different audiences and different timing. Choose the one that suits your location and kitchen, and start there.

2. Create a weekday reason

Guests need a reason to come on Tuesday instead of Saturday. A weekday-only experience usually protects margin better than a blanket discount:

  • A set lunch built for a one-hour break
  • An early-week menu or chef’s counter with limited seats
  • A wine or cocktail pairing night
  • Private dining packages for teams and client dinners

3. Reach people who are close by

Weekday decisions are local and last-minute. Concentrate on people who live or work within easy reach:

  • Meta campaigns targeted by radius around the venue, the nearby office districts and residential buildings
  • Google Search and Google Maps for people already looking for “lunch near me” or your cuisine in your area
  • A complete Google Business Profile with menu, hours and a booking link

4. Time it to the decision

Publish weekday content from Sunday to Tuesday, and schedule lunch advertising for mid-morning, when people decide where to eat. Invite creators on weekdays so their content shows the room as weekday guests will find it.

5. Go after groups

One team dinner can equal several tables. A simple enquiry form and a lead campaign for private dining can fill early-week evenings. For Town Hall, Sukhumvit 49, lead campaigns for venue hire produced more than 100 qualified enquiries in three months.

6. Measure weekdays on their own

Track covers, bookings, calls and direction requests for Monday to Thursday against the weeks before the campaign. If weekends are included, a good Saturday will hide what is happening on Tuesday.

FAQ

Common questions

Do weekday discounts work?

They can bring volume, but regular guests may start waiting for them. A weekday-only menu or experience gives a reason to visit without lowering the value of the full-price menu.

Which channel should we start with?

Start with Google Maps and Search, because they reach people who are already looking for somewhere to eat nearby. Add Meta campaigns to create the reason to choose you.

How long does it take to see a difference?

Bookings and enquiries usually show the first signs. Give a new weekday offer several weeks of consistent content and advertising before judging it against the earlier baseline.
