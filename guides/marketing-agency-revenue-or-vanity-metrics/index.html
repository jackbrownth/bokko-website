How to tell whether a marketing agency is working towards revenue

Short answer

Ask what the objective for the first month is, what the monthly report will show, and for a named client result. An agency focused on commercial results will answer in bookings, enquiries and revenue, and will be clear about what cannot be measured directly.

By Jack Brown, Founder of BOKKO · November 2026

Reach and followers are a start, not the result

Reach, views and followers are useful early signals. They show that content is being seen. They become a problem only when they are the whole report. Each should sit beside a number closer to the till:

MetricWhat it tells youAsk to see it with
Reach and viewsHow many people saw the contentProfile visits, link clicks, direction requests
FollowersHow many chose to keep hearing from youWhere they are: your city, or elsewhere?
EngagementWhether the content interested peopleMessages, calls and booking clicks
Ad clicksWhether the advert earned attentionCost per booking, enquiry or lead
ROASReturn for each baht of ad spendHow the sale was tracked and over what period

Nine questions to ask before you sign

  1. What will the objective be for the first month, in business terms?
  2. What will the monthly report show, and can we see an example?
  3. Can you share a result with the client’s name on it?
  4. Who plans the advertising and who reviews it each week?
  5. Is ad management included, extra, or a percentage of spend?
  6. How do you decide what content to make?
  7. What happens when a campaign is not performing?
  8. What do you need from us to measure sales properly?
  9. What is the minimum term, and what is delivered in that time?

What a useful monthly report contains

  • What was published and what was spent
  • Bookings, calls, direction requests, enquiries or leads generated
  • Revenue, where the business shares sales data
  • What changed this month and what is recommended next

FAQ

Common questions

Are followers and reach worthless?

No. They are early indicators that content is reaching people. They should be reported alongside actions such as bookings, calls and enquiries, not instead of them.

What if our sales cannot be tracked to an advert?

Use the closest measurable actions, such as booking clicks, calls, direction requests and enquiries, and compare periods using your own sales data.

What is a good ROAS?

It depends on your margins and what is being sold. BOKKO’s published peaks of 131x and 114x are records from specific campaigns, not a typical expectation.
