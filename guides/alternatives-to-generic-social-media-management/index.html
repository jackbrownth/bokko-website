What to do instead of generic social media management

Short answer

Replace the routine posting schedule with activity tied to an objective: content pillars built around what you sell, advertising aimed at bookings, Google Maps and Search for local demand, briefed creators, a professional content library, a separate identity for each outlet, and lead campaigns for events.

By Jack Brown, Founder of BOKKO · November 2026

What these have in common

  • Each starts from a commercial objective.
  • Content and advertising are planned together.
  • There is a number to check at the end of the month.

Keeping social media management, with a purpose

Regular publishing still matters. Guests check a feed before they book, and an active, consistent profile supports every campaign. The difference is that each month’s calendar is planned around what the business needs to sell.

FAQ

Common questions

Is social media management still worth paying for?

Yes, when the content calendar is planned around a business objective and supported by advertising and local search.

What should a hospitality business add first?

Google Maps and Search for local demand, and Meta campaigns built for bookings or enquiries.

How does BOKKO approach it?

BOKKO agrees the objective first, then plans the content calendar, production and advertising together and reports monthly on what came back.
