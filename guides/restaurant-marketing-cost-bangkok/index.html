How to set the advertising budget

There is no single right figure. Work back from the result you need:

  1. Decide the target in business terms, such as extra covers a week or event enquiries a month.
  2. Estimate what one new booking is worth to you, using your average spend per table.
  3. Agree what you are prepared to pay to win that booking.
  4. Start with a test budget, measure the cost per booking, call or enquiry, and increase spend on what returns.

What to check when comparing quotes

  • Is advertising management included, extra, or a percentage of spend?
  • How many shoots are included, and who owns the content?
  • How many pieces are published each month?
  • Who plans and reviews the advertising?
  • What does the monthly report show?
  • What is the minimum term?
