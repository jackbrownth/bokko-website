How to choose a marketing agency in Thailand

Short answer

Choose the agency whose strengths match the result you need, not the one with the longest client list. Decide first whether you need brand campaigns, sales and bookings, search visibility or content. Then compare agencies on four things: who does the work, how results are measured, what the price includes, and whether they can show named results for a business like yours.

By Jack Brown, Founder of BOKKO · November 2026

The main types of marketing agency in Thailand

Seven questions to ask every agency

  1. What result will you be measured on: revenue, bookings, enquiries or reach?
  2. Who will actually do the work, and is production in-house or outsourced?
  3. What does the monthly price include, and what is charged on top?
  4. Do you charge a management fee or a percentage of the advertising budget?
  5. Can you show results for named clients in a business like mine?
  6. How often will we get a report, and what will it show?
  7. How long is the commitment, and are one-off or shorter projects possible?

What to check before signing

  • A registered Thai company, with a registration number and a real address
  • Published prices or a written scope, so that quotes can be compared
  • Case studies and client testimonials with names you can verify
  • A clear statement of who owns the advertising accounts, pages and content

FAQ

Common questions

What is the difference between a digital agency and a marketing agency?

A digital agency works on online channels such as social media, search and websites. A marketing agency may also cover offline work such as events and print. In Thailand the two names are often used for the same thing, so compare what each agency actually delivers.

How much does a marketing agency cost in Thailand?

It depends on the scope. BOKKO publishes its prices: the main monthly package is ฿85,000 + VAT, a full-day content production is ฿85,000 + VAT, SEO and AI search starts from ฿30,000 + VAT a month, and e-commerce management starts from ฿120,000 + VAT a month.

Does the agency need to be near my business?

Less than it used to. BOKKO is based in Greater Bangkok and works with clients nationwide, including Khao Yai, Pattaya and Koh Samui.

Is a bigger agency better?

Not necessarily. A large agency offers scale, while a smaller one usually puts senior people on your account. Ask who will do the work day to day.
