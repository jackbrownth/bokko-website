Hospitality marketing glossary: ROAS, KOL, covers and the other terms agencies use

Short answer

ROAS is the sales tracked from advertising divided by what the advertising cost. Ad spend is the money paid to Meta, Google or TikTok, separate from an agency’s fee. A KOL is a creator or influencer paid or invited to feature a venue. The table below defines these and the other terms used in proposals and monthly reports.

By Jack Brown, Founder of BOKKO · November 2026

Advertising and measurement

TermWhat it meansWhy it matters
ROAS (return on ad spend)Sales tracked from an advertising campaign, divided by the cost of the advertising. A ROAS of 10x means ฿10 in tracked sales for every ฿1 spent on ads.It ties advertising to revenue. Ask what counts as a sale, and over which period.
Peak ROAS and average ROASPeak is the highest return reached by a campaign or in a period. Average is the return across the whole campaign.A peak figure shows what was possible; the average shows what was typical. A report should say which one it is quoting.
Ad spendThe money paid to the advertising platforms, such as Meta, Google and TikTok.It is separate from the agency’s fee, so budget for both.
Ad management feeWhat an agency charges to plan, set up and run advertising. Some charge a percentage of ad spend, others a fixed fee.A percentage fee rises with your budget. BOKKO includes advertising management in its package and charges no percentage of ad spend.
Performance advertisingPaid campaigns judged on a business result, such as bookings, enquiries or sales, and adjusted from that result.It keeps spending tied to an outcome you can count.
Meta adsPaid advertising on Facebook and Instagram, bought through Meta’s advertising system.One account and one budget cover both Facebook and Instagram.
ConversionThe action you want a person to take: a booking, an enquiry, a purchase or a call.Campaigns can only be optimised towards what is being tracked.
LeadAn enquiry from someone who could become a customer. A qualified lead fits what the venue offers, such as the right date, group size or budget.For private events and venue hire, qualified leads matter more than the total number of enquiries.
Reach, impressions and engagementReach is the number of people who saw something; impressions is the number of times it was shown; engagement is likes, comments, shares and saves.They describe attention. On their own they do not show whether revenue changed.
Vanity metricsNumbers that look good in a report but are not linked to a business result, such as follower counts or views.Ask how each number in a report connects to covers, bookings or sales.

Content and social media

TermWhat it meansWhy it matters
Content calendarThe month’s plan of what will be published, where and when, agreed before the month starts.It lets the venue approve the direction once, in advance.
ReelA short vertical video for Instagram, Facebook or TikTok.BOKKO’s monthly package includes at least four short-form reels a month.
Mood board and shot listThe mood board sets the look of a shoot; the shot list is the planned set of photographs and clips.They make sure a shoot produces what the campaigns need.
Creator, influencer and KOLPeople with an audience who feature a venue in their own content. KOL (key opinion leader) is the term most used in Thailand.Fees are normally paid to the creator, separately from an agency’s coordination fee.
Google Business ProfileThe free listing that shows a venue on Google Search and Google Maps, with hours, photos, menu and reviews.It is where guests find a venue’s hours, directions, photos and reviews.

Restaurant and hotel terms

TermWhat it meansWhy it matters
CoversThe number of guests served in a service, a day or a month.Weekday covers and weekend covers should be measured separately.
Direct bookingA booking made with the hotel or restaurant itself, through its website, phone or messages.No commission is paid to a booking platform, and the venue keeps the guest’s details.
OTA (online travel agency)A booking platform that sells rooms on a hotel’s behalf and takes a commission.Direct bookings avoid this commission, which is why many hotel campaigns aim for them.
RetainerA fixed monthly fee for an agreed scope of work, usually with a minimum term.Compare what is included: shoots, number of posts, advertising management and reporting.
VATValue added tax, 7% in Thailand, added to service fees.Check whether a quoted price includes it.

FAQ

Common questions

What is a good ROAS for a restaurant or hotel?

There is no single figure. It depends on margins, the average spend per guest and what is tracked as a sale. Compare ROAS with your own break-even point, and look at the average across a campaign as well as the peak.

What is the difference between ad spend and an agency fee?

Ad spend is paid to the advertising platforms. The agency fee pays for the people who plan, produce and manage the work. BOKKO’s package fee includes advertising management, with no percentage taken from the ad budget.

What does KOL mean?

KOL stands for key opinion leader. In Thailand it is the usual term for an influencer or creator who features a venue or product for their audience.
