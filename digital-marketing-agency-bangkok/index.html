How our digital marketing work is structured

Strategy before channel selection

The starting point is your business model. A restaurant may need more dinner reservations on weekdays. A hotel may want to grow direct enquiries and demand for specific packages. A new concept may need awareness before performance campaigns can convert.

We map audiences, differentiators, campaign messages, landing or booking paths, asset requirements and the measurement plan before deciding where budget should go.

An integrated marketing system

Our work spans social media management, content production, Meta, Google and TikTok advertising, creator campaigns and e-commerce growth on Shopee, Lazada and TikTok Shop. Combining these disciplines keeps the message consistent as people move from discovery to consideration and enquiry.

A single campaign might involve social-first reels, an Instagram profile, a Google search ad and a focused landing page. Each element should reinforce the others instead of competing for attention.

Commercial measurement, not vanity reporting

Reach and engagement have value, but they are rarely the whole objective. We prefer a small number of useful measures chosen for the business: qualified leads, enquiries, reservation actions, purchase revenue, cost per result, store visits or direction requests where supported.

The quality of tracking matters. Analytics, ad-platform events, lead form data and offline sales records often need reconciling, and we distinguish directly attributable conversions from indicators of interest.

Digital marketing agency questions

What does a digital marketing agency do?

A digital marketing agency plans, produces, runs and evaluates online marketing. At BOKKO that means creative production, social media management and paid media working as one programme.

Can BOKKO handle strategy, content and ads together?

Yes. Running those three together is the centre of how we work.

Do you work with international brands in Thailand?

Yes. We are Bangkok-based and our portfolio includes international hospitality names such as Mandarin Oriental Bangkok. Language, market and operational requirements are agreed during scoping.

How are marketing results reported?

Reporting is tied to the agreed objective, such as qualified enquiries, bookings or revenue, with supporting channel indicators alongside.
