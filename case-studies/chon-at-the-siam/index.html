The brief

Set within the Michelin Three-Key The Siam hotel, CHON sought to establish a distinct identity beyond the hotel itself—building awareness among Bangkok residents and visitors while increasing restaurant discovery, footfall and reservations.

What BOKKO did

BOKKO developed a refined brand and content direction that brought CHON’s authentic Thai cuisine, heritage setting and Chao Phraya riverside location to the forefront. We established clear content pillars, produced premium visual assets and activated targeted Meta and Google campaigns designed to reach high-intent dining audiences and convert online discovery into map visits and table reservations.
