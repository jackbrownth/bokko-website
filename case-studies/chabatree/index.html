The brief

Known for thoughtfully designed wooden kitchenware, tableware and home décor, CHABATREE required a complete social media transformation that better reflected its craftsmanship, natural materials and commitment to functional, sustainable design.

What BOKKO did

BOKKO established a refined creative and visual direction for CHABATREE, supported by professional photography, video production and considered brand storytelling. Through structured content planning, copywriting, publishing and ongoing social media management, we transformed the brand’s digital presence into a more cohesive and premium showcase of its products, craftsmanship and design philosophy.
