The brief

Located within Rancho Charnvee Resort, Chez Sonia required a distinct digital identity and customer-acquisition strategy to attract diners beyond the resort’s resident guests.

What BOKKO did

BOKKO developed a dedicated creative direction for Chez Sonia, supported by professional photography, short-form video and ongoing social media management. Content highlighted the restaurant’s Italian cuisine, atmosphere and resort setting, while Meta, TikTok, Google and Google Maps campaigns targeted local residents and travellers planning visits to Khao Yai. This combined content and performance strategy increased restaurant discovery and encouraged visitors to include Chez Sonia within their Khao Yai itinerary.
