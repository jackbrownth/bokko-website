The brief

Town Hall required a cohesive content and marketing strategy to establish the newly opened Sukhumvit 49 community mall, showcase its mix of vendors and services, and generate commercial enquiries for its Haus of K event venue.

What BOKKO did

BOKKO managed Town Hall’s social media presence and produced ongoing photography, video and branded content highlighting the mall’s tenants, dining concepts, services and community experiences. Alongside organic content management, we developed targeted Meta lead-generation campaigns for private events and venue hire at Haus of K, delivering more than 100 qualified leads within three months.
