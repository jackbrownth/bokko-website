The brief

As a SET-listed restaurant group with a growing portfolio of established and emerging dining concepts, MAGURO Group requires premium creative work that can elevate individual brands while maintaining the standards and credibility of the wider group. MAGURO Group operates across premium Japanese dining, sushi, Korean barbecue and Japanese shabu and sukiyaki concepts.

What BOKKO did

BOKKO supports MAGURO Group with creative direction and high-quality content production for established concepts and new brand launches. We develop photography, video and campaign key visuals designed for use across social media, restaurant interiors and customer-facing digital screens. Each production is tailored to the individual concept, creating a distinctive and desirable brand identity while delivering versatile assets that remain consistent across every customer touchpoint.
