The brief

Renaissance Pattaya required a stronger visual presence that more effectively communicated the resort experience to its target markets while creating versatile assets with long-term value.

What BOKKO did

BOKKO developed an elevated creative direction and produced premium photography and short-form video showcasing the resort’s accommodation, beachfront setting, pools, dining venues and guest experiences. The new content strengthened the overall quality and consistency of the social feed while providing the hotel with a versatile evergreen asset library for ongoing use across social media, digital campaigns and wider brand communications.
