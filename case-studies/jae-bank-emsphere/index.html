The brief

Opening at Emsphere in Bangkok, Jae Bank required a complete creative and performance strategy to establish its identity, build immediate awareness and drive customers to the new restaurant.

What BOKKO did

BOKKO developed the complete social media concept and brand direction for Jae Bank, creating a distinctive visual identity supported by original photography, video and campaign assets. We combined brand content with selected influencer and KOL partnerships, Meta advertising, Google Ads and Google Maps campaigns to maximise launch awareness, strengthen local discovery and convert online interest into restaurant visits.
