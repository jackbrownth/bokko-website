The brief

As a newly opened Spanish restaurant in Bangkok’s Surawong district, Tasca Sabio required a complete launch strategy to establish its identity, build immediate awareness and attract customers from across the surrounding business and hospitality area.

What BOKKO did

BOKKO developed an integrated launch and growth strategy combining creative direction, professional photography and video production, social media management, creator appointments and performance advertising. Content introduced Tasca Sabio’s authentic Spanish tapas, drinks and lively dining atmosphere, while Meta, TikTok, Google and Google Maps campaigns targeted nearby residents, office workers, expatriates and international visitors. This multi-channel approach supported immediate restaurant discovery while building longer-term awareness for planned dining occasions and group visits.
