The brief

Aesop’s required a stronger visual identity for social media—one that captured the energy, atmosphere and personality of the venue while giving potential guests a compelling reason to visit.

What BOKKO did

BOKKO developed an elevated creative direction and produced professional photography and short-form video designed specifically for social media. The content showcased Aesop’s food, drinks, rooftop setting and lively dining experience, transforming its social presence into a more distinctive and desirable representation of the brand.
