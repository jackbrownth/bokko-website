The brief

Trot Café required a destination-led marketing strategy that would attract travellers already visiting Khao Yai while building future travel intent among audiences in Bangkok.

What BOKKO did

BOKKO developed a distinctive creative direction supported by professional photography, short-form video and ongoing social media management. Content showcased the café’s country setting, atmosphere and experiences, while Meta, TikTok, Google and Google Maps campaigns reached both active Khao Yai visitors and Bangkok audiences considering future trips. This combination of immediate customer acquisition and longer-term destination awareness helped establish Trot Café as one of Khao Yai’s most recognised café experiences.
