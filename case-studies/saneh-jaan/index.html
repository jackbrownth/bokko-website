The brief

Awarded a Michelin star for nine consecutive years, Saneh Jaan sought to elevate its digital presence, reach new local and international audiences, and translate its culinary reputation into sustained restaurant demand.

What BOKKO did

BOKKO delivered an integrated restaurant marketing strategy combining elevated creative direction, professional photography and video production, social media management, creator appointments and performance advertising. Meta, Google and Google Maps campaigns were used to capture immediate dining intent while building awareness for future visits, celebrations and special occasions. Content and targeting were developed to reach Bangkok residents, expatriates and international visitors seeking an acclaimed Thai fine-dining experience.
