The brief

With restaurants in Bangkok and Krabi, Bistro di Farina required a coordinated content and performance strategy capable of strengthening both locations while reaching distinct local and visitor audiences.

What BOKKO did

BOKKO developed and managed a multi-location restaurant marketing strategy combining professional photography and video production, ongoing social media content and performance advertising. Separate content was produced for the Bangkok and Krabi venues to reflect each location, atmosphere and target audience. Meta, TikTok, Google and Google Maps campaigns were continuously optimised to reach local residents, domestic travellers and international tourists, contributing to strong revenue growth across both restaurants.
