The brief

Anantasila sought to elevate its social media presence while establishing its beachfront restaurant as a dining destination for Hua Hin visitors beyond the hotel’s resident guests.

What BOKKO did

BOKKO developed the resort’s ongoing content direction and produced professional photography and short-form video showcasing its accommodation, beachfront setting, facilities and dining experiences. We managed content planning, copywriting, publishing and social media activity to create a more cohesive digital presence. Alongside the hotel’s organic content strategy, targeted Google Ads campaigns reached travellers actively searching for places to dine in Hua Hin, successfully driving a substantial increase in restaurant guests from outside the hotel.
