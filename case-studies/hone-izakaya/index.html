The brief

Located in Sukhumvit 24, HONE required a complete restaurant marketing strategy to establish its premium positioning, attract local and international dining audiences, and build consistent customer demand.

What BOKKO did

BOKKO delivered a full-service restaurant marketing strategy combining creative direction, professional photography and video production, social media management, creator appointments and performance advertising. Meta, Google, Google Maps and TikTok campaigns were used to capture immediate dining demand while also building awareness for future occasions, celebrations and planned visits. Campaigns targeted Bangkok residents, expatriates, international tourists and visitors searching for premium Japanese dining experiences in the Sukhumvit area.
