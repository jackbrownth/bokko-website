The brief

Amara Bangkok sought to strengthen its digital identity through more refined visual content, clearer brand storytelling and a consistent social media presence that better reflected the hotel experience.

What BOKKO did

BOKKO established an elevated creative direction for the hotel’s social channels, supported by professional photography, short-form video and ongoing content management. Through structured content planning, copywriting, publishing and platform management, we created a more cohesive brand presence while showcasing the hotel’s rooms, facilities, dining experiences, rooftop setting and central Bangkok location.
