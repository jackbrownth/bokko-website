The brief

บู๊ลิ้มหม้อไฟ เมืองโบราณ required a complete restaurant growth strategy to strengthen its digital presence, attract new diners and convert online visibility into sustained sales growth.

What BOKKO did

BOKKO delivered a full-service restaurant marketing strategy with complete control over creative direction, photography and video production, ongoing content management, creator appointments and performance advertising. We developed content that highlighted the restaurant’s hot pot offering, atmosphere and dining appeal, while Meta, TikTok, Google and Google Maps campaigns targeted local audiences and diners actively searching for restaurants in the area. This integrated content and customer-acquisition strategy helped increase sales by more than 250% within four months.
