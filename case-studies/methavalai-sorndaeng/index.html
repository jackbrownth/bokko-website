The brief

After more than five decades of serving authentic Thai cuisine, Methavalai Sorndaeng sought to attract younger local diners and international visitors while preserving the heritage and character that define the restaurant.

What BOKKO did

BOKKO developed an updated creative direction supported by professional photography, short-form video and refined social media content. We presented the restaurant’s cuisine, historic setting and enduring character in a more contemporary and visually engaging way, helping Methavalai Sorndaeng appeal to younger Bangkok audiences and foreign tourists without losing the authenticity, tradition and brand recognition built over more than 50 years.
