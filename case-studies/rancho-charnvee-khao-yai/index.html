The brief

Rancho Charnvee required a more refined and consistent social media presence to showcase the breadth of its resort, country club facilities and destination experiences.

What BOKKO did

BOKKO developed the resort’s ongoing content direction and produced professional photography and short-form video tailored to its social channels. Through structured content planning, copywriting, publishing and account management, we created a cohesive digital presence that highlighted the property, facilities and guest experience while strengthening Rancho Charnvee’s positioning as a distinctive Khao Yai destination.
