The brief

Located within Rancho Charnvee Resort, Piman Terrace required a distinct social presence that positioned the venue as a destination in its own right, rather than simply an extension of the hotel.

What BOKKO did

BOKKO developed a dedicated creative direction for Piman Terrace and produced professional photography and short-form video showcasing its drinks, food, atmosphere and resort setting. Through structured content planning, copywriting, publishing and ongoing social media management, we built a cohesive identity designed to increase awareness among resort guests, Khao Yai residents and visitors to the destination.
