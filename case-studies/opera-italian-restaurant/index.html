The brief

Opera required a complete restaurant marketing strategy to strengthen its positioning, reach new dining audiences and convert increased digital visibility into sustained revenue growth.

What BOKKO did

BOKKO developed a cohesive creative and performance strategy combining professional photography and video production, ongoing social media management and multi-channel advertising. Content showcased Opera’s cuisine, atmosphere and authentic Italian dining experience, while Meta, TikTok, Google and Google Maps campaigns targeted Bangkok residents, expatriates and international visitors. Through continuous content development and campaign optimisation, the restaurant tripled its monthly revenue by the fourth month of the engagement.
