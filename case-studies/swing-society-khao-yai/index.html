The brief

As a newly opened driving range and leisure destination, Swing Society required a complete launch strategy to build awareness and attract golfers, families and travellers visiting Khao Yai.

What BOKKO did

BOKKO developed Swing Society’s launch direction and digital presence through professional photography and video production, ongoing content planning and social media management. Performance campaigns introduced the venue to Thai and international visitors, local residents and active golfing audiences, while destination-led content positioned Swing Society as an experience to include within a Khao Yai itinerary.
