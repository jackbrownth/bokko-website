FAQ

SEO and AI search questions

How much does SEO cost with BOKKO?

A dedicated SEO and AI search programme starts from ฿30,000 + VAT per month. The scope is set after an audit of the site and covers technical SEO, content, Google Business Profile and AI search visibility, in English and Thai.

What is AI search optimisation?

It is the work of making a brand easy for AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Mode and Perplexity to find, understand and cite. It relies on clear facts on the website, structured data, consistent company details across the web and independent sources that confirm them.

Is AI search optimisation different from SEO?

They share the same foundations. SEO aims at rankings on a results page; AI search optimisation aims at being named and cited inside an answer, which puts more weight on direct answers, verifiable facts and consistent information across sources.

Can BOKKO guarantee a number-one ranking?

No agency can. Rankings and AI answers are decided by the search engines and AI providers. BOKKO works on what influences them and reports what changes.

How long does SEO take to show results?

It depends on the site and the competition. Technical fixes take effect as search engines re-read the site; rankings and AI mentions for competitive searches build over months.

Does BOKKO do SEO in Thai as well as English?

Yes. Pages, structured data and profiles are prepared in both Thai and English.
