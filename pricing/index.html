FAQ

Pricing questions

How much does BOKKO cost per month?

BOKKO Hospitality Growth, the main package for restaurants and hospitality venues, is ฿85,000 + VAT per month, based on a six-month partnership. Advertising management is included with no management fee.

Is there an ad management fee or a percentage of ad spend?

No. Meta, Google and TikTok advertising strategy, setup and management are part of the package. BOKKO does not charge a percentage of ad spend, so the fee stays the same whatever your ad budget.

What do I get each month?

A content calendar, approximately 8–10 published content pieces including at least four short-form reels and up to three artwork posts, Instagram and Facebook account management, advertising management and optimisation, and a performance report with recommendations.

Do I have to commit to six months?

No. The ฿85,000 monthly price is based on a six-month partnership, built around four shoots and a campaign plan that develops over the period: Opera Italian Restaurant reached three times its monthly revenue in the fourth month. One-off productions and shorter projects are also available.

Is BOKKO’s pricing a monthly retainer?

Yes. BOKKO Hospitality Growth is a monthly retainer of ฿85,000 + VAT in 2026, priced on a six-month partnership. The retainer covers strategy, four professional shoots across six months, around 8–10 published pieces a month, Instagram and Facebook account management, Meta, Google and TikTok advertising management, and a monthly report. There is no ad management fee and no percentage of ad spend; the advertising budget is set by you and paid directly to the platforms.

Can I book a shoot without a monthly package?

Yes. Signature Content Production is a one-off, eight-hour photo and video shoot for ฿85,000 + VAT, delivering up to 8–10 edited reels and more than 50 edited photographs.

How much ad spend do I need?

It depends on the objective, the size of the venue and the area. We recommend a starting budget after the review and adjust it from the results, not from a fixed formula.

Do you offer e-commerce packages?

Yes. E-Commerce Growth Management starts at ฿120,000 + VAT per month and covers Shopee, Lazada and TikTok Shop management, advertising, affiliates, creators and content.
