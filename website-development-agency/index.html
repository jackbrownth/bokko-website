What website development with BOKKO covers

Design and front end

Pages designed in the brand’s own style and built to load quickly and work properly on a phone, where most visitors arrive.

Back-end systems

The systems behind the pages: content management so your team can update the site, enquiry and lead handling, and the integrations the business needs.

Landing pages for campaigns

Pages built for a specific advertisement: one offer, one action, and a form that sends the enquiry to the right place.

Thai and English

Two language editions with a switch between them, so Thai and international visitors each read the site in their own language.

Tracking and measurement

Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, the Meta Pixel and advertising conversion tracking, so campaigns can be optimised against real enquiries and sales.

Ready for search and AI

Clean page structure, structured data and sitemaps from the start. See SEO and AI search optimisation.

Process

How a website project runs

1. Brief

We agree what the site has to do: the actions that matter, the pages needed and the systems behind them.

2. Design

Page designs are prepared for phone and desktop and approved before development starts.

3. Build

Front end and back end are developed, with content, forms and tracking in place.

4. Launch and measure

The site is tested, launched and measured, so the next changes are based on what visitors do.

FAQ

Website development questions

How much does a website cost with BOKKO?
