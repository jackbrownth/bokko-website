FAQ

Creative and production questions

Does BOKKO have its own production team?

Yes. Creative direction, photography, film and editing are handled in-house by BOKKO’s production team, so the people who plan a campaign are the people who shoot it.

Is BOKKO a creative agency or a performance agency?

Both. BOKKO develops and produces the creative in-house and runs the advertising that uses it, so the work is judged on revenue as well as on how it looks.

What does a full-day production include?

Signature Content Production includes a creative concept, a mood board, a production plan and shot list, an eight-hour photo and video shoot with creative direction, up to 8–10 edited short-form reels and more than 50 edited and graded photographs. It is ฿85,000 + VAT.

Which brands has BOKKO produced creative work for?

Published work includes CHON at The Siam, Chabatree and Swing Society, and Rongros Dining Group’s Contento and Lucky Duck Bar.

Can the content be used in advertising as well as on social media?

Yes. Assets are delivered in formats for social media, advertising, websites and brand campaigns.
