How we plan and shoot content

The concept comes before the camera

The process begins with the objective and the customer: what must the viewer understand or feel, and what should happen next? We develop references, a concept board, a shot list and a practical production plan around the available time and venue.

This avoids a common problem: beautiful imagery that does not answer the commercial brief or fit the campaign placements.

Photography that expresses the experience

For restaurant photography, food matters but so do people, service and atmosphere. For hotels, a room image may need context: arrival, views, dining, wellness or the neighbourhood. For lifestyle brands, the emphasis could be product use, texture, craftsmanship or personality.

We plan a variety of compositions and crops so assets work on a website, a social feed, a story or an advertisement.

Reels and short-form video built for attention

Short-form video needs a clear opening, a strong visual idea and economical editing. The narrative should fit the platform without sacrificing the brand.

We plan reels as a regular content system or as part of a specific launch, menu, offer or advertising programme. Deliverables and the production schedule are set by the project.

Assets are part of the growth system

Content is most valuable when it is published with a coordinated message and tested against an objective. Production, social management and paid advertising inform one another: campaign results guide what gets shot next.

Because production sits alongside our social and paid media work, campaigns are planned as one programme.
