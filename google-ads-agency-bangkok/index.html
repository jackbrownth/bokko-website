How we plan and run Google campaigns

Match paid search to real intent

Someone searching for a restaurant by name has different intent from someone exploring dinner options. Someone looking for a hotel package is different from someone reading about a destination. Campaign structure and copy should reflect that difference.

We group terms by service, audience and action, then write ads that set the right expectation. Negative keywords and location choices keep irrelevant visits down.

A useful landing page matters

Getting a click is only the beginning. An ad should lead to a page that answers the question, shows credibility and makes the next action obvious. Slow, confusing or mismatched pages make otherwise good campaigns expensive.

We review the destination alongside the ad copy, including mobile experience, forms, calls, booking links and measurement tags.

Choosing the right Google campaign types

Depending on the objective and available assets, options include Search campaigns and Performance Max. They are not interchangeable: the right choice depends on tracking quality, conversion signals, creative assets and budget.

Reporting separates platform-attributed conversions from independently confirmed business outcomes wherever possible.

Optimise over time, not by assumption

We review search-term quality, spend, geographic patterns, audience signals, creative relevance and conversion outcomes. Decisions wait for enough data to avoid reacting to random daily variation.

Google advertising, including Search and Maps, is part of our standard package alongside Meta and TikTok. Account access, conversion configuration and management scope are agreed before launch.

FAQ

Google Ads questions

Does Google Ads put a website at the top of organic search?

No. Google Ads placements are paid and separate from unpaid organic rankings. SEO is a different workstream.

Do you manage campaigns in English and Thai?

Yes. Language requirements are scoped per campaign, including ad copy and landing-page localisation.

Can you track calls and enquiries?

Yes, where it is technically feasible and permitted. We then check tracked actions against the quality of actual enquiries.

How does the advertising budget work?

You set the budget and it goes directly to the platform. Ad management is included in the monthly fee, with no management fee and no percentage of the budget.
