This page explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. Information we collect

We collect information directly from you when you fill out our contact form, specifically:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your business or brand name
  • What you're looking for help with (selected from a dropdown)
  • Any additional details you choose to write in the message field

We do not collect payment information, government ID numbers, or other sensitive personal data through this site.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses Google Tag Manager, which we use to run Google Analytics (GA4) and Google Ads conversion tracking. These tools use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Understand how visitors use this site (pages viewed, general location, device type)
  • Measure which marketing campaigns lead to enquiries, so we can understand what's working

This data is aggregated and used for analytics purposes — we don't use it to individually identify you beyond what you directly tell us through the contact form. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or block cookies generally through your browser's privacy settings.

How we use your information

We use the information you submit through the contact form solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Understand your business and recommend the right service or package
  • Follow up about a potential or ongoing working relationship

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties, and we do not use it for purposes unrelated to responding to your enquiry.

Third-party services we use

Running this website involves a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf:

  • Netlify — hosts this website and processes contact form submissions, which are then forwarded to our email.
  • Google Firebase — stores and delivers the content shown on this site (case studies, images, page text).
  • Google Tag Manager, Analytics and Ads — used for site analytics and to measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns, as described above.
  • Behold.so — powers the embedded Instagram feed shown on this site; this may load content directly from Instagram/Meta when you view that section.

Each of these providers has its own privacy practices governing data they process on our behalf.

Data retention

We keep contact form submissions for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry, maintain business records, or as required by law — after which they're deleted.

Your rights

Under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), and similar laws that may apply depending on where you're located, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for us to contact you

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@bokko.co and we'll respond as soon as we reasonably can.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for business use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our website or practices change. The "last updated" date at the top will always reflect the most recent revision.

Contact us
